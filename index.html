Our mission

Dashly Campus exists to help CWRU students get more value out of their meal plans and stop overpaying for Uber Eats and DoorDash just to eat on campus.

You already pay for campus dining through tuition and meal plans — delivery shouldn’t require a second wallet. Our goal is simple: bring your favorite campus food directly to your dorm, reliably and affordably, powered by students who actually live here.

How Dashly works

We’re starting small and keeping it simple while we pilot. Here’s what the student experience looks like.

1. You order from Jolly

Pick your favorites, tell us where you live

You’ll order your favorite Jolly Pizza / Jolly Scholar items and select your dorm & preferred time window. Orders are limited during the pilot so we can learn and stay reliable.

2. A runner picks it up

Student runners assigned by “backyard”

Dashly runners are CWRU students assigned to specific backyards (North, Middle, South) so pickup is fast, predictable, and built around each Jolly location.

3. We meet you at your dorm

5-minute window, then back to Jolly

We text you when we’re on the way and meet you at your dorm entryway. If you can’t meet us within 5 minutes, your order goes back to the Jolly location for pickup. During the pilot, we’re learning what works now while we work toward warming stations and, eventually, secure lockers.

Backyards & delivery windows

Instead of trying to serve the entire campus at once, we’re piloting by “backyard” — tight delivery zones around each Jolly location.

North backyard · Jolly Pizza

Focused on the freshman-heavy side of campus.

  • All freshman dorms
  • STJ & The Village
  • Greek housing excluded during the initial pilot

North will be our first focus so we can prove the model where demand is already strongest.

Middle backyard · Jolly Scholar

Serving the center of campus once we see strong traction.

  • Hessler housing
  • Apartments above Plum Market
  • Twin Gables, 1716, and nearby housing
  • Greek housing will be added later

Jolly Scholar deliveries will open to more students as we prove reliability and volume with the first zones.

South backyard · Southside Scholar

Focused on the hill and upper-class housing.

  • Top of the hill housing
  • Bottom of the hill housing
  • Greek housing excluded in early pilot

Southside comes online once we’ve shown we can consistently serve North and Middle without overextending runners.

Delivery windows (pilot draft): We’re currently exploring late-evening blocks such as:
• Weeknight test blocks between 8–11 PM • Weekend blocks between 8 PM–12 AM Final windows will be announced to pilot signups as we coordinate with Jolly staff.

Why we’re starting with Jolly

Jolly Pizza and Jolly Scholar are already part of CWRU culture. Dashly is simply building a way to get them to your dorm without a third-party app.

Student favorite, now delivered

We chose Jolly because students already love it. Giving Jolly a delivery layer is the fastest way to test whether campus-native delivery can work without changing what people order.

First to offer campus-run delivery

Jolly is stepping up as the first dining partner to let students build and run a delivery pilot on campus. Their role is simple but critical: make the food, trust students to handle the last mile.

“Win the backyard” first

Jolly’s team talks about “winning the backyard” — proving we can reliably serve the area around each Jolly location before trying to cover the whole campus. Once each backyard works, we expand delivery windows and reach.

For students

Use the value you already pay for
